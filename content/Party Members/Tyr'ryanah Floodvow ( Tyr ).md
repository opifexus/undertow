### Backstory

The night of a viscous plane-wracking storm - the night the sea turned red - was the night Tyr’ryanah was born into a pod of Azarketi. She would not be long with them. 

Azarketi, by their very nature are a varied and complex race, both culturally and physically, but even Tyr’s birth parents would never claim her as their child. A sign of doom was born with her - a glowing green orb, tear shaped and tiny to begin with, known to seafolk as “The Eye of [[The Maelstrom|the Maelstrom]]”. 

Knowing of her fate, of the sign that tainted her birth, her pod agreed unanimously on two actions concerning to infant. One - they would raise her until a survivable age as an outcast and then formally exile her, and Two - she would not be welcome to return. 

The horns on her head began as two small, green-scaled nubs that were six inches long by the time she was exiled. They grew faster than she did. So, too, did the green swirling orb of chaos above her head and the agitation that came the closer she got to The Maelstrom. Her Pod agreed to exile her earlier than expected, at age six. She never knew their love or acknowledgement, only their guidance and their fear.

For many years, more than she knows now, she was alone after that. Never knowing the embrace of a family or safety of a true Pod gave her an advantage - loneliness was never her enemy. In fact, she delighted in exploring solo once she learned to move with the currents, making them a part of her. Once she was confident in surviving alone, the vastness of the ocean became more of a challenge than anything else.

As a young one, she stuck to the sea floor and the wrecks that slept there. Most were picked over, clean, but she soon learned to find those places that no one else ever thought to look, and treasures began to abound. These were not the treasures some may expect - forks, pocket watch chains, beads and coins, stone tools, and anything and everything that looked strange and unusual. 

She collected and traded these items with passers by, plowing her trade and learning how to negotiate, to deceive, to haggle. She was excited when she found she was quite good at it, and even more so when she began to meet those who did not flinch at the sight of her. She began to make games for herself to see how much she could get someone to tell her before they realized she was more savvy than she seemed. To this day, Tyr considers false naivete her ultimate weapon, and she employs it actively whenever she can. 

As a teenager, she became enamored with the origins of her birth, the reason for her exile and the magic that coursed through her body and her spirit. Tyr began seeking knowledge of this, but to no avail. Very little has been gleaned, though this is and always has been her true focus. 

It never stopped distractions from pinning the young Azarketi to a trench wall on occasion. During one excavation, she was attacked by a IDK SOME KIND OF SCARY WATER MONSTER. Though she managed to fight the beast off, she sustained fatal wounds that her meager attempts at first aid were unable to patch. She knew she was dying, and the only comfort was that she would not live to see the End of the World.

But she did not die. She awoke in the care of a small group of beings - Selkies, they called themselves. They found Tyr on the brink of death and, living by their creed, they saved her life by passing along their Beastkin nature. Each of the Selkies was made in this manner, and they consisted of aquatic beings of many varieties. Those who chose to stay, to call themselves Selkies, remained in their Hybrid form exclusively, believing this to be their True Skin. Those who were given the gift and chose to wear their Old Skin (or worse, became enraged upon waking up, forcing retribution) would be asked to move along once their training was concluded. 

The Matron of them, another older Azarketi by the name of Thetis, took Tyr under her wing. During the time they were together, Thetis shared many secrets of their Beastkin heritage and abilities, but she also shared her hatred of the surface world and the mortals that populated it. Tyr absorbed her teachings, honing her skills in her Hybrid form and combat alike, but that was all she chose to take in. When she felt her training had reached its zenith, after only two weeks, she left The Selkies to continue her search.

It was after this time that she met Pearl, her little sister. On an expedition, she found Pearl inside of a clam shell, alone and with no one to protect or guide her. She immediately adored her, adopted her, named her and began calling her “Little Sister”. Pearl is the only family that Tyr has ever known, and she guards that bond fiercely. 

She seeks out knowledge of ancient algothulu culture, believing them to be the source of the evil that plagues her. Further, she is convinced that the Maelstrom is linked directly to the forces that keep her people hostages to their machinations. Despite her treatment by all of her people, and even some outside of Azarketi culture, she still wishes to discern the nature of their relationship with their once-masters. Her ultimate goal is to sever her connection with the Maelstrom, and her power, ultimately thwarting the prophecy that binds her to a future of drowned death. Her true wish is for all people to be free to be and live as they choose, and she will stop at nothing to see this goal through to its end.
