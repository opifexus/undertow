
### Relationships


Toys ‘n Trinkets

Ren’s shop is tiny. It has a main storefront and basic workbench setup up front. And in the back he has his real workshop, where he manufactures all illicit goods. Also, he has a small sleeping quarters connected to his workshop, so canonically his workshop is also his house.

  
Ren

One noticeable backstory point is that he opened his shop with good intentions, but there’s only so much of a market for toys and trinkets in a place like Na Keung. His shop inevitably was not profitable, leading to dipping his toes into nefarious doings. This has been clarified in the past, but the Halim Marauders tasked Ren with making a spectacular weapon. Once it was finished, Ren couldn’t part with it. Not only did he fear what would happen if it fell into the wrong hands, he felt connected to it. It was his masterpiece. Specifically, he was meant to deliver this weapon to Vorrak on a deadline. Ren was pushing the deadline further and further back, and eventually he fled on the Rascal Razorfin. Could potentially help explain why they were confronted when initially buying/boarding the ship.

  

Brie Boffin and Asher “Ash” Boffin (Ratfolk)

Brie and Ash are Ren’s parents. Notably Ash is an avid rollerblader. Ash was a mechanic in Na Keung and Ash a seamstress at the Temple of the Mother Serpent. Both are retired now in their older years and they reside in a humble home in Kajang. Ren is in good standing with both of his parents. There are no noticeable conflicts or unresolved situations.

  

Lilly (Ratfolk)

Lilly has served as an assistant at Toys 'n Trinkets for a few years under Ren. She mostly handles simple storefront tasks such as running the register, entertaining customers, and keeping the shop tidy. Notably she is younger than Ren and she looks up to him as a mentor. Not having any experience in crafting, she yearns to one day master carpentry and mechanics at Ren’s level. When Ren left on the Rascal Razorfin, he vanished hurriedly without paying a proper goodbye to Lilly. Ren has always been fond of Lilly as an assistant and student of the craft. He never made her privy to his private doings, and worked hard to protect her from that scene. He has no reason to believe she knows anything about it. After leaving, he regrets not paying her a proper goodbye or explaining the situation. His best guess is to assume Lilly believes he got into some trouble or something bad happened to him.

  

Vessara (Nagaji)

Vessara works at the Mother Serpent temple Na Keung. This temple is where Ren began to use his excess wealth to craft and donate toys to children of the temple, as well as performing tasks for their community in his field (building a bench, fixing floorboards, etc.). Notably she began as a patron of his shop and turned into a friend after many visits, which lead to the relationship between Ren and the church.

  

Illoy

Illoy works at the Mother Serpent temple in Perna. After Ren’s donations to the temple in Na Keung became plentiful, he sought other avenues for community outreach. Vessara referred Ren to Illoy. Illoy visited Ren’s shop and Vessara helped build their relationship. Ren began crafting and donating toys to Illoy’s temple.

  

Bart

Bart is a crew member of the Rascal Razorfin. Before that, he and Ren had a history in smuggling. Bart was a friend of Ash, unrelated to Ash’s line of work. Bart was sort of an Uncle to Ren growing up. Bart was aware of Ren’s natural talent for crafting. When Ren’s shop fell on hard times due to lack of customers, Bart timidly offered Ren another revenue source. Ren was looking to expand his crafting knowledge and was in need of a way to keep his shop afloat, so he started to work with Bart and his contacts on many projects. Notably smuggling, illegal weapons manufacturing, and thievery. Maybe Bart joined the crew to protect Ren, as he felt guilty introducing him to this dangerous lifestyle.

  

Vorrak (Hobgoblin) and Torg (Ogre)

Vorrak and Torg are members of the Hamil Marauders. Vorrak is a mercenary that specializes in hand to hand combat, and Torg is an enforcer who is known for his immense size and grotesque appearance. They often work as a pair and are considered high-level veteran members of the gang. Ren was introduced to them through Bart. Once Ren had done enough business with them, they began to pressure Ren by “offering” protection. These kinds of agreements are not uncommon in Na Keung, manufacturers are often “protected” by one of the gangs in Na Keung. The deal is that the Halim Marauders provide Ren’s shop with protection and stand his shop as their “turf”, and in return Ren and the shop receive the benefits of the gang’s intel and protection from other gangs messing with him. They appointed Ren a Watchman named Bastian.

  

Bastian (Orc)

Bastian is the appointed “Watchman” for Toys ‘n Trinkets. He himself is not a member of the Halim Marauders, but was hired by them as a third party. You can think of him as sort of a protector and security force for Ren and the shop. He’s not necessarily an employee of the shop either. He keeps eyes and ears out to make sure that Ren and the shop don’t get messed with or intimidated by any rival gangs, and often will accompany Ren on any dangerous drop offs. Although appointed as a Watchman by the Halim Marauders, Bastian does not associate with them. He is not evil or bad by nature. He and Ren have a good relationship, and Ren views him fondly. Maybe not necessarily as a close friend, but at least an enjoyable coworker. Ren does not feel intimidated by him and is appreciative of the length he goes to provide protection. Bastian may be confused why Ren left, but not surprised. He knows the business Ren has gotten into.
