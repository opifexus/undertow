15 years prior, Araya was hired as the Phantom Hand to plant incriminating evidence in the captain’s quarters of a political rival. While she takes these sorts of jobs with great care, one of the crew happened upon her while she was on her way out. He was a fascinating creature, some aquatic sort of orc judging by his blue complexion and singular, prominent tusk. Taking a gamble on the younger man’s loyalties, Araya advised him to jump ship before it sank. Sure, she was telling the truth, but her hope was that he’d allow her to slip away without making a fuss. It would also help take the heat from her heist; the Phantom Hand had stopped leaving a calling card some time ago, but a deckhand gone AWOL is a much more likely explanation for an emptied safe than an urban legend. 

  

She was right to bet on the orc.

  

As expected, news of the aforementioned captain’s arrest, and gossip of his inability to post bail, made its way to Coron a few days later. While Araya enjoyed the chatter of Encoron’s pub, a familiar face caught her eye, just as she seemed to catch his. Curious, she called over the man who nearly blew her cover and paid for his drink on the captain’s dime. He told her his name was Pepek. He had a strong build - hard to overlook on account of his exposed chest and arms and his chiseled jawline - and seeing as to how he was freshly out of work due to her actions, Araya invited him to stay with her and her family for a while. 

  

Araya was taken by him, and it didn’t take long to discover that the attraction was mutual. What’s more, this developing romance seemed to breathe new life into Sarick, the first thing to successfully pause his 30 years of bleaching.

My dearest Frior,

  

There has been much excitement on the Rascal since we parted ways in Perna, and while I know you were called home for good reason, my heart selfishly yearns for your company. Perhaps one day we may try to adventure again, when my sister no longer requires assistance.

  

There have been some changes to our crew, most notably a Nagaji by the name of Sathona. She is one of whom I am becoming fond, though I fear your morals may not take so kindly to her occupation. Additionally, we have gained a sister of Lyra’s by the name of Caelynn- a shock to her, much less the rest of our party. We met their mother as well, but that is a story best heard, not read.. 

  

I can however share with you our most recent exploit. We are currently anchored in Durango, having just returned from travel south to Ardhi. While Durango has not changed much since my last stop in its ports, our current dealings with the Bantu Trading Company have been quite fruitful - suffice it to say, my share should cover Papi’s cleric visits for a comfortable while. 

  

We headed from Durango to Ardhi on Bantu’s commission - a lovely city rich in art and culture - and found ourselves assisting the governing authorities in investigating an assassination by none other than those who accosted us in Perna. This led us to explore a place below known as  the “Under.” For the sake of brevity, I will summarize that portion of our journey with the knowledge that these particular assassins should not be pursuing us further.

  

While our return back to Ardhi from the “Under” was uneventful, the return to Durango was much more exciting. We encountered nothing less than an entire stampede of Kodos being pursued by none other than a Purple Worm! It was a treacherous escape to be sure, as Sathona was struck by its stinger, but I managed to single-handedly distract it by striking down a Kodo, and it happily chose the easier prey. 

  

Our party retreated all the way to the coast, and its members were quite excited to see the ocean once more, as was I. Tyr and Sathona practically raced to the water. While I managed food and fire, Lyra and Caelynn spent much time that evening in contemplation and Ren did some tinkering -he surprised us the next morning with a beautiful handcrafted replica of the Rascal! I plan on asking him to craft some figures for Leif, as I’m sure he would be thrilled to have more soldiers for his marine corps. Tyr and Lyra had quite the lengthy conversation at one point. I am quite sure it involved Lyra’s current family bonds, perhaps Tyr’s as well - they have both gone through so much. I must find time to check in on those two.

  

That night spent camped on the beach was truly euphoric. I deeply wish you could be here to experience this with me.

  

We have since returned to our ship in Durango and will soon be reconvening with Bantu to close out our commission. I also overheard Tyr and Sathona’s voices in the captain’s quarters while passing by - perhaps I should add Sathona to my list of conversations.

  

Come to think of it, Ren also mentioned having some familial strain… I am starting to worry that my heart is being pulled as it did in my days on the Capoeira. Perhaps I should have used a different name when joining this crew.

  

I fear this journey may be longer than I anticipated. My hope is to return before Lief’s next birthday, but we are now bound by the arcane to a task that will take an inestimable amount of time. Until then, I will endure the ache of having you, Ana, and Lief so far, and will continue to dream of when I will next be in your embrace.

  

Yours eternally,

  

Araya
