## Sathona and Tyr's Conversation
- Sathona visits Tyr in her private quarters to thank her for saving her from the axi omdal and the worm.
- Tyr expresses her gratitude for Sathona making it to her so she could help.
- Sathona confides in Tyr about her fear during the encounter with the worm.
- Tyr comforts Sathona and assures her that she is glad she made it to her in time.
- Sathona expresses her fear of dying alone and uncared for, but is grateful for the companionship and support of her friends.
- She reveals her past as an orphan who survived by stealing and eventually became a skilled assassin.
- Sathona decides to change her ways and embrace a new life with her newfound friends.
