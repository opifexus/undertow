## The Key to the Maelstrom
- The adventurers met with Karu and Muan from the Society of Pathfinders to discuss their next move.
- They learned that the machine is located in a demi-plane and they need a key to access it.
- The Pathfinders offered them membership in the society, which includes signing an NDA, participating in initiation trials, and gaining access to their services.
- The group received a ring of sending to communicate with the Pathfinders and a folded origami letter that can get them out of a sticky situation.
- They discussed the information with Arana and Sathona, who suggested that the key could be hiding in plain sight and easily accessible.
- Sathona believes that the key might be something inconspicuous, like a jeweled pin, and that it could be in the possession of someone like Remington.
- The group agrees that finding the key won't be the issue, but getting into the heavily guarded demi-plane will be the challenge.
- The key to the Maelstrom could be anything from a physical object to a phrase or a person.
- The more complex the key, the more difficult the magic required to create it.

## The Awakened Mavish Crystal
- The awakened Mavish Crystal needs to be taken back to the Maelstrom, but the party is unsure if they are strong enough to face the challenges there.
- The party decides to perform a ritual to awaken the crystal further, hoping that it will enhance the ship's capabilities.
- Ship Spirits are intelligent creatures of thought flesh that serve their nation and inspire the crew.
- They usually appear as glamorous humanoid women and can be recalled by embedding the mishan crystal into the woodwork of the vessel.
- The group discusses the best course of action to perform the ritual to summon the Ship Spirit.
- They decide to head to the temples of the wind and waves deity, Alasbos, to strengthen their connection before performing the ritual.
- The temples are located in Perna, the southernmost tip of Tulig, and OKO.
- They choose to go to OKO first as it is equidistant and allows more time to pass since their encounter with Yousef.

## Political Landscape and Course of Action
- The group discusses the political landscape of Sita, the capital and seat of the monarchy, and the splinter governments and groups within the continent.
- They agree to head straight for Valencia, Tulig, despite Lyra's limited knowledge of navigating the cities and populace.
- The group decides to chart a course for Valencia, Tulig, and consider changing their flags before setting off.

## Ship's Flag Design
- The group is discussing the flag they should fly on their ship.
- They decide not to fly the Tabian flag because they don't want to be associated with the Tabian Traders.
- They consider creating their own flag but decide it would be easier to fly a neutral flag.
- They decide on a flag with a kraken on it to represent their ship, the Rascal.
- They choose a dark navy blue color for the flag so that it doesn't look too fancy and attract pirates.

## Octopus Plush Toy Design and Shopping
- The group is discussing the design for an octopus plush toy.
- They decide on a white octopus with dark blue for the background and interlocking tentacles with no face.
- The total cost for two of these plushies is 45 gold.
- They then discuss shopping for items in the game.
- The shopping list is in the "references" channel on Discord.
- The group is currently level five, so they can buy level five and level six items.
- Some of the items they consider include the Venom spell, the Primeval Mistletoe spell, the Kri, Ghoul Hide armor, the Shadow Rune, the Lion Shield, the Staff of Evocation, the Spellguard Shield, and various runes.
- Lyra is trained in light and medium armor, so she can wear medium armor.
- The group helps Lyra choose the best armor for her, and they decide on the Ren Hide armor.
- The party discusses their finances and decide to contribute 16 Platinum to the party fund.
- Lyra is interested in purchasing the Clandestine Cloak for its stealth and deception bonuses.
- The group considers various armor and rune options, including Silver and Cold Iron armor, Presentable Rune, Rune of Returning, Diplomat's Badge, and Diplomat's Sherry.
- Saona expresses interest in a Blood Red Giant Curved Knife but cannot use it yet due to her lack of training.
- The group discusses selling their items for half price and Lyra decides against purchasing the Presentable Rune since she can cast Prestidigitation.
- The party discusses purchasing striking runes for their weapons and armor.
- They consider the benefits of the Shell of Easy Breathing, which allows a creature to breathe underwater for an hour, but it has a once-per-day limit.
- The Lifting Belt is discussed, which grants a +1 item bonus to Athletics checks and increases carrying capacity. It is decided that Seta should have it.
- They decide to purchase the Armor Potency Rune and the Shadow Rune for their armor.

## Securing their Money

- The party discusses where they keep their money on the ship and consider ways to secure it.
- They decide to hide the chest with their money in the Captain's Quarters and set up a trigger to alert them if someone enters the room.
- They decide to take a short break and then continue their shopping session before ending the session before 10 pm.
