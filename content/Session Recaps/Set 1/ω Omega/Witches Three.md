## The Serpent and the Brood Mother

- Before going to the market, the group met with an intriguing man named Dr. Viasei, also known as the Serpent, who possessed a specific set of skills but did not have the stones they were searching for.

- After a hike, the group encountered a terrifying manticore called The Brood Mother, also known as Sat, who offered prophecies to some members of the group.

## Pakarang and the Circle of Chlam

- The group then traveled to Pakarang, a settlement characterized by tidal pools and coral structures, where they were greeted by Schlam, one of the witches of the Circle of Chlam.

- The population in the area includes gnomes, halflings, humans, elves, and jasi.

## Schlam and the Circle of Chlam

- Schlam came to Talite to study and was outcast from a college in the Wisdom Hall, but it is not clear which one.

- Schlam, Beanie, and Rockley are dressed comfortably for the season.

- Brockley suggests that being a witch is a unique experience.

- Satai is described as the "brood mother" who grants powers to the witches.

- Satai has the ability to see the threads of fate, a power that was not easily acquired.

- Bei lost a close friend which led her to seek out a Patron.

- Satra is a weaver and a speaker of fates.

- Most witches find comfort only in the company of other witches.

## The Shadow Circles and Other Magical Activities

- There are a couple of Shadow Circles in Uung.

- Saona has knowledge of the underworld and can provide information about individuals involved in illicit activities, such as hiring witches to curse someone.

- There are rumors of unusual magical activities happening in Kami and Shambala.

## Potions and Magic

- A potion called "Wine of the Blood" offers healing, euphoria, and enhanced resistance to negative emotions and effects for one minute.

- There are potions that have various effects, including shrinking and enhancing acrobatics skills.

- An individual's ability to become a witch depends on their openness to receiving power from a patron and whether they have the capacity for it. Some individuals might possess innate magic or have lineages that prevent them from forming pacts with patrons.

- Rockling is skilled in potions.

## The Maelstrom and Toia Ships

- The Melstrom, a storm with a cyclical seasonal pattern, has been exhibiting unusual behavior, with changes in intensity, pace, and potential movement, according to Beim, who has studied it extensively.

- Toia ships have been repositioned north recently.

- Amy shakes her head when asked if she thinks the Maelstrom was a mistake made when the world was created.

## Lyra and Her Magic

- Lyra is a magic user whose magic no longer works. She left a society of magic users after her Arcane magic failed.

- Lyra is from a Buian family of light magic users.

- Lyra's familiar is an orange that she puppeteered. Despite not moving on its own, the orange emanated an aura or presence that could be sensed.

- Lyra grew up in a place where she did not have access to the outside world and spent most of her time in a library.

- Lyra approaches the bed and finds it similar to a carousel.

- Lyra is content with the information she has learned from the witches.

## Pakarang and its Inhabitants

- The land in Pakarang is open to those who treat it with respect.

- A short, elderly halfling with a druidic appearance served a delicious floral-flavored gelon from a giant pot.

- There were two sleeping options: hammocks rotating on a water wheel and more formal bedding inside a lush area with coral groves and bioluminescence.

- Rockley offered to discuss and exchange spells and formulas with Lyra over drinks. Lyra accepted water instead of wine.

## Witches and Their Perception

- Witches are viewed negatively by others because they are seen as taking shortcuts to learning magic.

- An individual who grew up in a wealthy family felt like a black sheep because they didn't initially possess magical abilities.

- This individual eventually discovered a way to obtain magical powers through research, which led to their estrangement from their family.

- This individual is now embracing their identity as a witch and is eager to learn more about their capabilities.

## CU's Encounters

- CU is searching for a specific plant. A friend explains that the plant's seed pods are roasted as a delicacy in South OKO, but touching the plant causes a rash.

- Bei believes that the god responsible for the storm is Alasaboro, also known as Gazra, the wind, and the waves.

- Bei's notebook contains a sketch of the storm with an odd, irregular shape underneath it that is neither a creature nor a recognizable form.
