- The adventurers return to the Bank of Durango to receive payment and further instructions from the Bantu Trading Company.
- They meet with [[Rivington]], a Stout Dwarven representative, who discusses their completed tasks and introduces them to the Pathfinder Society.
- Rivington informs them that they need to collaborate with [[The Pathfinder Society]] to locate a specific [[the occult machine|machine]].
- The Pathfinder Society is a secretive organization that employs adventurers and investigators for noble causes.
- [[Rennet Boffin ( Ren )]] is familiar with their headquarters in Absalom.
- Rivington suggests meeting with two Pathfinder Society members in Durango to discuss further business arrangements.
- As payment, Rivington hands them a folded check before leaving the room.
