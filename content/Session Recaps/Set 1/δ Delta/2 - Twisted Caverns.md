- The party discusses their findings from their investigations in the city.
- Sathona shares information about job opportunities for mercenaries and assassins.

- They decide to head to the Rusty Hook to meet up again and discuss their next move.
## Encounter with Kayla
- On their way, they encounter Kayla and prepare to leave for their journey.
- A party member expresses surprise at not knowing about Kayla's existence and asks about their mother's involvement.
- Kayla confirms that she has had dream conversations with their mother, while the party member has not.
- They discuss the details of their shared dream, including a waterfall, caverns, a staircase, an archway, darkness, a storm, and a distant light.
## The Strange Place
- The group fell through a light and ended up in a strange place.
- They encountered a being who questioned why they were there.
- They proceeded through cavernous tunnels that shifted and changed form.
- They encountered an oculi, a nasty Fay creature that plays with people's fear of the dark.
- Araya is very uncomfortable and has had nightmares about oculi in the past.
- The group stealthily attempted to skirt along the wall, but the oculi turned its head towards them.
