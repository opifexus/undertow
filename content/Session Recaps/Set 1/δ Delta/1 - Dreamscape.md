## Dream Experience
- The adventurers find themselves in a shared dream within a cavernous interior with a waterfall and a spiraling staircase.
- Lyra attempts to fly but fails, and the group discusses the nature and significance of the dream.
- Lyra learns through a religion check that shared dreamscapes are uncommon and often associated with cults.
## Maelstrom and the Cult
- The group discovers that they are being pursued by a cult that intends to use them to intensify the Maelstrom, a massive storm brewing in the North Center Ocean.
- The Maelstrom is prophesied to flood the world, and the cult believes the group is somehow connected to the prophecy.
- Despite their magical abilities, the group's magic is not functioning properly within the Dreamscape, except for divination magic.
## Beam of Light
- Sana ascends the staircase and enters a doorway, finding herself in a dark void.
- Sana, Ren, and Saana enter the void and discover they can see each other but nothing else.
- Sana continues forward and sees a beam of light in the distance, which the group believes is the source of the Maelstrom.
## Mysterious Voice and Offer
- As they descend towards the light, the slope becomes steeper, causing them to slide down and eventually fall into the Maelstrom.
- They end up in a strange, dark place where a mysterious voice addresses them, offering them a chance to fulfill their potential.
- The voice explains a problem that needs to be solved and that the group is needed to help.
## Waking Up and Decision
- The group wakes up back in their beds after falling from a great height and discusses their experience.
- They debate whether to seek more information or go directly to the mysterious voice.
- They decide to spend a few hours gathering information from the townspeople before confronting the voice.
