## Dealing with the Hag
- The party negotiated with Mother, who appeared to be Lyra's mother, to release Kalin from her curse.
- Kalin was reluctant to give up more of her life to the hag, leading to a tense discussion among the party members.
- A deal was struck with the hag to travel to the Seas through the Maelstrom and give her missing crystals in exchange for releasing Kalin and sharing information about the agulu and the cult.
## Exploring the Hag's Lair
- While exploring the hag's lair, Ara and Siona discovered a lampad, which was Kalin's real biological mother, and a pool of power with an agulu in it.
## Journey to the Priest
- The group decided to head to the priest in Arty to update him on their findings.
- They considered taking the lake route but decided against it due to time constraints and swimming abilities.
- They opted to go back through the sewers, which they were more familiar with.
- Araya successfully shoots a rope with a crossbow to the pipe entrance, allowing them to climb back up.
## Seeking Information from the Priest
- The group visited the Church of Foras to speak with Racer.
- They informed him about their investigation of the Foreign Affairs Minister's home and the involvement of Lazaro, who has been removed from the investigation.
- Racer expressed concern about Lazaro's removal and the INF taking over the investigation.
- The group received a message from a woman named LOL, who offered to arrange other business if they meet in Durango.
- Racer agreed to try sending a message to Lazaro to inform him of their return and potential departure the next day.
## Group Discussions and Reflections
- The group discussed their sleeping arrangements in the college dorm-style room.
- They reflected on the recent revelations about the Crystal and its true nature, considering its ethical implications and potential harm.
- They speculated on the possible locations of the remaining temples dedicated to [[Alassabouros]] and acknowledged the importance of temples as places of connection with the gods.
- Lyra and Tyr had a long conversation before falling asleep, with Lyra expressing her anxiety about her dreams and how they used to provide clues from her mother.
## Preparations for Departure
- The group discussed their plans for returning to Durango and the supplies they need.
- They decided to visit the bathhouse before leaving town so Tiar can submerge.
- They considered sending a letter to Lazaro but were concerned about his safety and whether he could receive messages.
- They decided to send a magical message to Lazaro through a familiar cleric but did not receive a reply.
## Journey to Toer Submerged
- The group headed to the lake outside the city for safety and planned to try to enter Toer Submerged.
- Araya offered to share traveling stories with Kayn to help pass the time during their journey.
