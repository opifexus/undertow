## Exploring the Sewers
- The players defeated a gelatinous cube and found various items, including the Conundrum Spectacles and a toy dagger.
- Ren, covered in ooze, and the party decide to heal up and discuss their marching order.
- Ren and Tyr successfully navigate the sewers, finding a grate blocking their exit.
- Sathona fails to pick the lock due to a distraction, but Arah succeeds.
- The party descends down a precarious walkway using a rope.
## Underwater City
- The party discovers the city is underwater and needs to find a way down.
- Lyra casts Dancing Lights to illuminate a tube leading underwater, and the party dives in.
- They see lights and buildings as they approach the entrance to the underwater city.
- To cross a 60-foot underwater tunnel, Sathona swims with one end of a 100-foot rope while Lyra casts water breathing on the other end.
- With guidance from Mariah, Sathona successfully swims across, surprising the group with their speed.
## Cavern Town
- The party finds themselves in a cavern with a small town inhabited by various races.
- Arah disguises herself as a human, and the party decides to take a short break before exploring further.
