## Krajcik's Involvement
- The party finds [[Krajcik]]'s establishment in the Undercity and speaks with them.
- [[Krajcik]] confirms knowing Lazar and agrees to help the party in their investigation.
- [[Krajcik]] suggests a subtle approach in meeting with the people they seek.
- [[Krajcik]] offers information in exchange for a price.
- Araya proposes a business partnership with [[Krajcik]], which [[Krajcik]] accepts.
- [[Krajcik]] provides information leading to the person the party needs, who is also the person [[Krajcik]] needs killed.
- The party agrees to the terms and plans to procure an assassin for the job.
## Mysterious Entity and Potential Target
- The group encounters a mysterious entity known as "aaga" and is offered an "Arcane bargain" to kill an unknown target.
- Lyra rolls a natural 20 on a perception check, hinting that the target might be Lyra's mother, a hag.
- The group discusses the potential consequences of making a deal with aaga and the risks involved in killing a powerful hag.
- They consider seeking more information before committing to the task.
## Hag Contract Negotiations
- The group tries to determine if the person they are speaking to is leading them into a trap.
- They offer to do a smaller job or provide more information before accepting the assassination contract.
- The person they are speaking to is evasive and does not provide much information.
- The group eventually agrees to pay 150 gold pieces for information about the target.
- The person gives them a riddle about the hag's location and tells them to seek out her followers.
- The group hesitates and tries to negotiate further, but the person refuses to give more information.
- The group declines the contract.
## Arcane Bargain with aaga
- The group makes an Arcane Bargain with aaga to kill an individual named Kin Kalin.
- Kin is described as a pale elf woman with long white hair, bright violet eyes, and markings on her neck.
- The group is given a general location but no further details.
- The Arcane Bargain has a time limit of one week before they are compelled to complete the task.
## Meeting Lyra Melena
- The group finds a large establishment that appears to be a tavern or meeting place.
- The tavern is mostly populated by nagaji, and the group stands out as foreigners.
- They meet Lyra, a pale Elven woman with violet eyes and long white hair, at a table on the balcony overlooking the pool.
- Lyra recognizes the player character and seems to know a lot about them, but the player character does not recognize her.
- Lyra reveals that she is the player character's sister, and that their mother has kept them in the dark about each other.
- Lyra welcomes the player character and their friends to the city and assures them that they will be safe there.
- The player character asks Lyra many questions, including why she is here and why they are all here.
- Lyra responds that they are all here by a twist of fate and that they should not diminish their own meaning or purpose.
- Lyra agrees to take the player character to see their mother.
