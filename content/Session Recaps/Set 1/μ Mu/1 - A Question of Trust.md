- The adventurers set sail towards the Temple of Alasbos in Valencia to conduct a ritual to awaken the Mavisian crystal while at sea.
- They discuss Mother's revelation that the ship spirit inside the crystal may possess valuable information.
- The group decides to gather privately to share relevant information, including a dream experienced by one adventurer.

## Automatons and Sentience
- The adventurers consider the possibility that the ship spirit or an automaton like Lucky might qualify as a sentient being not bound by the laws of the universe.
- They roll to determine their knowledge about automatons and discuss the nature of sentience and whether copies or imprints of personalities can truly be considered new consciousness.
- The group considers rumors and whispers surrounding the creation of sentient automatons and the potential political and financial implications.
