## Infiltration of the Hookah Lounge

- The group discusses their plan to infiltrate a hookah lounge to gather information about the Pathfinder Society.
- They decide to split into two teams: a diplomacy team and a disguise team.
- Lyra, Ren, and Tyr will be on the diplomacy team, while Sathona and Araya will be on the disguise team.
- The disguise team will arrive first, disguised as customers, and eavesdrop on the Pathfinder Society's meeting.
- The diplomacy team will arrive later and engage in conversation with the Pathfinder Society members.
- The group discusses the details of their plan, including how to signal to each other and what to do if things go wrong.
- They also discuss the possibility that the Pathfinder Society members may be able to detect their intentions.
- Tyr expresses concern about splitting up the group, but they ultimately agree to the plan.
- The group heads to the hookah lounge and begins their mission.

## Gathering Information about the Machine
- The party discusses their plan to gather information about the machine from a contact in a public space.
- They decide to disguise themselves as Dwarf and Elf and sit in a booth near a hallway leading to private rooms.
- They review the information they have gathered so far, including the involvement of [[Youssef Morata]] and [[Lord Mido Cassalanter]] in the machine's construction.
- They decide to ask their contact about their intentions, plans, and how they located the machine.
- They agree to decide what information to disclose based on Charisma and diplomacy during the conversation.
