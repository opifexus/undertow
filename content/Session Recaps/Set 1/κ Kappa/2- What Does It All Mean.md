## Muen and Karu Meeting

- The group meets [[Mwenye]], a beetle-like person, and [[Kifaru]], a vulture-like person, at a luxurious hookah lounge.
- Muen and Karu offer drinks and food to the group and express interest in getting to know them before discussing business.
- The group introduces themselves and mentions their openness to any kind of work.

## The Machine and the Artisans
- Muen and Karu reveal their involvement in various curious businesses and mention rumors about a machine causing a stir.
- The Pathfinders explain their mission to ensure unsolicited technological advancements don't fall into the wrong hands.
- The Pathfinders share information about the Artisans involved in creating the machine, including the Moratas, the Cassalanters, and Xavier Markov.
- The party possess blueprints and witness testimonies related to the machine and the Artisans.
- The Pathfinders have a personal investment in destroying the machine and seek allies.

## Investigation Roadblocks
- The Pathfinders encountered a dead end in their investigation at a certain household and were impeded by the authorities.
- They suspect Xavier Marov is the "X" mentioned in Yousef's notes and the caricature with the Rune-face tattoo.
- Lord Herel and Xavier Marov are implicated in the assassination of the Cassalanters.
- Lord Hershel and Lord Castaner met two months before Castaner's assassination.

## The Machine's Location and Clues
- The machine is being held in a Demi plane, which is difficult to track and likely heavily guarded.
- The blueprints for the machine were obtained from Yousef's study, but Yousef is dead.
- The party killed Yousef and cannot approach his family for information.
- The son of Lord Castaner, who is unaccounted for, may have some insight into the machine.
- The party wonders if the child depicted in the painting, who is the son of the artist Xanthu Maag, could provide information about the machine.
- Xanthu Maag is the artist who painted the painting with the initials "XM," the same initials as Xavier Marov.

## Group Discussion and Plans
- The group discusses the information gathered so far in their investigation.
- They speculate on the whereabouts of Luca Castander, Lord Castander's brother, who left the family and may have been involved in shady dealings.
- They identify several points of interest, including the residences of Xavier Marov and Lord Hershel, as well as merchants involved in delivering machine parts.
- They discuss the possibility of the machine being kept in a demi-plane and the different ways a demi-plane can be accessed.
- They decide to split up to investigate further, with one group focusing on researching the demi-plane and the other tracking down Markov and Lord Hershel.
- They discuss the fate of Chief Investigator Lazaro, who was taken off the assassination case and is now in decent spirits.
- They acknowledge the political nature of the investigation and the need to be cautious in their actions.
- They agree to use a sending ring to stay in communication with each other.

## The Pathfinders' Offer
- The Pathfinders offer the group a choice between signing an NDA, participating in an initiation ceremony to become official Pathfinders, or forming more formal business contracts.
- The initiation ceremony involves trials, and any information uncovered in the name of the Pathfinders must be reported to the society.
- The Pathfinders offer various rewards, including access to a global network of teleportation circles, magical trinkets, and exclusive information.
