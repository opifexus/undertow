## The Hag's Lair
- The party found the Hag's lair, which resembled a vivid and lucid dream.
- Lyra's mother appeared, offering them soup, which they consumed without harm.
- The Hag revealed their purpose of saving the world and explained that Tear's boat spirit was a fragment of a soul capable of persuading a god to stop a destructive magical storm.
- They learned about an "Ult Machine" designed to erase Ault Magic, angering the Hag.
## Caelynn's Past
- Araya suggested that Caelynn pay the price for removing the Arcane Bargain, leading to an emotional outburst from Caelynn.
- Caelynn confessed that she was not her first mother's child but was given away as a bargaining chip for a connection to natural ley lines and magic.
- The party sympathized with Caelynn and tried to understand why someone would want her dead, but she couldn't provide an answer.
## Exploring the Hag's Domain
- The party cautiously explored the hag's domain, unsure if she could overhear their conversations.
- They considered using Caelynn as a bargaining chip since the hag hadn't offered anything in return for their labor.
- Arah discovered a set of stairs leading down and decided to investigate with Saethona while the others created a distraction.
## The Seer's Well
- Descending the stairs, they found a large cavernous space with a lit pool and a humanoid figure lying on a stone altar.
- The figure was laying, who appeared to be asleep.
- Arah touched the water in the pool with the hag's permission.
- They encountered a pool of water and experienced strange sensations after making perception and will saving throws.
- They saw a figure resembling Kaitlin's face lying on a stone table with tears streaming from its eyes.
- Mother appeared and invited them to come down and speak with her.
- Caelynn hurriedly ran down the stairs, followed by the party.
- Mother revealed that the creature was a Seer's Well and that Kaitlin was being kept there for a specific purpose.
- Mother had made a deal with Kaitlin's mother to keep her alive in exchange for the Seer's Well.
- The party learned that Kaitlin's existence was irregular, resulting from an arrangement between two female lampads.
- Mother explained that if Kaitlin died, her deal would be broken, and she would be forced to try to kill Kaitlin within a week.
## Negotiations with the Hag
- The party negotiated with the hag for Kalin's release and the dissipation of the Maelstrom.
- The hag agreed to release Kalin and free her from the Arcane bargain if the group traveled to the Maelstrom, offered all the Maelstrom crystals they possessed to the wind and waves, and answered all of her questions about the alulu and the cult after Tyr.
- The group discussed the terms of the agreement and added additional requests, including an ongoing informational exchange regarding the dissipation of the Maelstrom and the alulu.
- The hag agreed to the terms, and the group finalized the contract.
- They ensured that the contract did not bind any of them individually and confirmed that they had only seen one Mavish and Crystal, the one in their possession.
