## The Cottage
- Inside the cottage, there is a cauldron brewing with a sweet-smelling liquid, possibly seafood soup.
- There are spiral stairs along the perimeter of the oval-shaped interior, and dried herbs, fruits, and meats hanging underneath.
- There is a wooden stove with cushions around it, but no other places to sit.
## The Figure
- A shadowy figure resembling Lyra's mother appears, descending a staircase.
- The figure is described as a pale woman with long dark hair, a witch's hat, and a royal gown.
- Rebecca and Lyra sense that there's more to the figure than meets the eye.
- The figure addresses the party, welcoming them and offering them food and rest.
- The party is served food in fine silver bowls, and Lyra notices that the figure is not looking directly at them.
- Lyra makes a perception check to see if the figure is looking at her, but the figure seems to be looking at Tear.
## The Prophecy
- The figure explains that the party's journey is unique and that they have brought something with them that is not hidden anymore.
- The figure mentions a "small storm" and "things" that are not hidden anymore, one of which the party is aware of.
- The figure instructs the party to go to the "center of the eye of it all" to find treasure that will help save the world.
- The figure mentions legends of the herald holding back the font of eternal youth and endless treasure at the center of the eye.

## Breaking the Arcane Bargain
- Lyra asks Mother about the cost of breaking the bargain.
- Mother says she can take a minute or a few seconds of Lyra's lifespan or own her for that amount of time.
- Lyra is hesitant to give up any of her lifespan, but she is willing to do it if it means saving Kayn's life.
- The group wants to know if there is another way to break the bargain without sacrificing Lyra's lifespan.
- Mother says there is another way, but she wants more information in exchange.
