## Players' Adventure
- The players continued their adventure in the Pathfinder campaign.
- They had a collective dream where they met Lyra's mother, who warned them about the end of the world and asked for their help.
- They woke up floating in midair and gathered information about the city and the missing people in the shifting caverns.
## Encounter with the Oculi
- The players encountered a terrifying creature called an oculi, which is transparent and about the size of a small chimp.
- Lyra sensed imminent danger and described the scene: they are on a walkway with a cliff face and a rope bridge nearby.
- The oculi is 50 feet away on a small alcove in the wall of the walkway.
- Sathona moved forward and successfully hit the oculi with her electric arc spell, dealing damage.
- Lyra learned more about the oculi from a nature check, discovering that they embody the fear of being lost and helpless in the dark.
- The players fought the oculi, which had disorienting and illusion-causing abilities.
## Battle with the AAL
- The players fought a creature called an AAL, a semi-aquatic predator resembling a demonic badger.
- Ren was grappled by the AAL and was in danger of being dragged off the cliff.
- Ryan cast Spiritual Weapon, creating a magical beam that struck the AAL.
- The AAL was defeated, and its illusionary corpse disappeared, revealing empty eye sockets.
## Fight with Horned Mermaids
- The players fought two horned mermaids.
- Ren was grappled by one of the mermaids and pulled away from the group.
- Lyra cast Spiritual Weapon on the mermaid attacking Aria.
- Sathona attacked the other mermaid with sneak attack, dealing significant damage.
- The mermaid grappled Sathona and pulled her closer, but she managed to avoid a critical hit from its jaws.
- Ren sustained his spiritual weapon and moved it to attack the creature.
- Aria cut off the snarling creature's tail, freeing Sathona, and then moved forward to attack it.
- Ren successfully hit the creature with his cannonball, dealing fire damage.
- Lyra sustained an attack with the spiritual weapon and hit the creature twice, dealing force damage.
- Sathona attempted to flank the second creature but missed both attacks.
- The second creature critically hit Sathona, but she survived due to her Nimble Dodge ability.
## Defeating the Badger Demon
- The players fought a badger demon.
- Ren used a megaton strike to finish off the badger demon, which was flung off the cliff and into the water.
- The players were exhausted from the fight but healed Sathona.
- They noticed a nest on the cliff side where the badger demons came from and decided to move on.
## Exploring the Nest
- The party discussed the possibility of finding missing body parts in the nest of the creatures that attacked them.
- They lowered Aria into the nest using a rope.
- Aria found several mutilated corpses and valuables, including a wand of lock, an obsidian necklace, and a coin purse.
- The party took a short break to identify the items and discuss their next move.
