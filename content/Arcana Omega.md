Chapter 0, IV, VIII, XII, XVI, and XX all take place in the same timeline and universe, a high-magic medieval fantasy setting. The Fool, The Emperor, Strength, The Hanged Man, The Tower, and Judgement tell one story. Carrie, a lady page, wins the chance to meet with the King of the realm. Despite Carrie's agoraphobia, she goes to the Royal Banquet and uncovers an assassination plot that she is quickly framed for. The King escapes and vouches for her, against the voice of his people. Seeing his kingdom crumble, the pair face the world anew.

Chapter I, V, IX XIII, and XVII all take place in the same universe and timeline, a Weird West setting of cowboys and eldritch horrors. The Magician, The Hierophant, The Hermit, Death, and The Star all tell this one story: A city-slicker named McCrary strolls into the western town. Attempting to sell insurance, he soon uncovers the town's traditions are a bit abnormal. He meets with the reclusive Sheriff who tells him to abandon all he knows as the pair uncover eldritch horrors. Will they lose hope against the unknowable?

Chapter II, VI, X, XIV, and XVIII all take place in the same universe and timeline, a futuristic setting aboard a broken spacecraft headed to colonize a new planet. The High Priestess, The Lovers, Wheel of Fortune, Temperance, and The Moon all tell this one story: Evelynn wakes from cryostasis 200 years too early to find herself alone with Adam, her husband. Seeing as they are the only two passengers alive, they must stabilize the broken spacecraft in order to survive and repopulate. After Evelynn becomes pregnant, it is revealed that Adam was illusory: a doppelganger took the form of Evelynn's husband in order to seduce and impregnate her.

Chapter III, VII, XI, XV, and XIX all take place in the same universe and timeline, a horrific hellscape of devilish lawyers and gamblers. The Empress, The Chariot, Justice, The Devil, and The Sun all tell this one story: A debt collector in the Hells, Karmylla, meets an unfortunate turn of events, and is backstabbed by the casino owner she works under. She seeks justice and makes a pact with one of the Lords of the Nine Hells in order to come out on top and eventually reach the surface.

Finally, the main characters from each setting meet to face the real challenge of AO, who has been manipulating each of their fates.
