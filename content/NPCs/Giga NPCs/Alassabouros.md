Alassabouros, or more commonly referred to as [[Alassabouros|The Wind and The Waves]], is a Diety of the primal forces of the ocean.

### Relationships

[[Alassabouros]] was formerly romantically intwined with [[The Man Lost To Time]]. Their relationship ended when they were gifted the knowledge of the [[Mavishan Crystals Lore|Mavishan Crystals]].
