The Occult Machine is a device crafted by [[Lord Mido Cassalanter]] and [[Youssef Morata]], as well as some splinter cell backstabbers from [[The Pathfinder Society]], [[Xavier Markov]]. Its intended purpose is to remove the Occult Threads from the Weave in order to disrupt the magicks of the [[Mavishan Crystals Lore|Mavishan Crystals]].  

It is currently being housed in a demiplane temporarily until its construction is complete. It will then be moved to Absalom (or [[The Maelstrom]], haven't decided) for its purpose to be actualized.
