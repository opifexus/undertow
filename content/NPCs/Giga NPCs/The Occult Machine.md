The Occult Machine is a device crafted by [[Lord Mido Cassalanter]] and [[Youssef Morata]], as well as [[Xavier Markov]]. Its intended purpose is to remove the Occult Threads from the Weave in order to disrupt the magicks of the [[Mavishan Crystals Lore|Mavishan Crystals]].  

It is currently being housed in a demiplane temporarily until its construction is complete.
