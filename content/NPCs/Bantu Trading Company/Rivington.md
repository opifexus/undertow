T.E. (Trader Extraordinaire) [[Rivington]] is a Stout Dwarven individual who welcomes the party and prepares to discuss business.

He offers the party knowledge of the secret society known as [[The Pathfinder Society]] who are vehemently against technological advances that benefit the few.

Under his employ in this way are [[The Pathfinder Society|Pathfinders]], [[Mwenye]] and [[Kifaru]] .
