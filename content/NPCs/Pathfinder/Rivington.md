T.E. (Trader Extraordinaire) [[Rivington]] is a Stout Dwarven individual who welcomes the party and prepares to discuss business.

He offers the party knowledge of the secret society known as [[The Pathfinder Society]].

Under his Bantu Trading Company employ in this way are [[The Pathfinder Society|Pathfinders]], [[Mwenye]] and [[Kifaru]] .
