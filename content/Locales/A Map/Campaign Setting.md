# Introduction

The mysteries of the oceans are as deep and dark as the waters themselves. These are the primeval birthing pits of the world, where creatures as ancient as the world’s heart still swim in hidden trenches. Vast oceans sprawl beneath the ground, with never a ray of sunlight breaking their murky waters. Civilizations formed and grew, suffered and died, and the bones of their cities still lie on the ocean floors. Life in the world of Urd is no different.  
  

## Tuligaña  (Too-lee-gan-ya) 

This is the land associated with the Spanish and Portuguese Naval Armadas. While their government in [[Cenita]] may want to claim they own the seas, the Tuligañeros are much more relaxed.  The Tuligañeros value innovation and creativity, valuing those who can bring new ideas and inventions to the table. They have a reputation for being relaxed and accepting, welcoming people of all backgrounds and ancestries into their society. The Tuligañeros are known for their skilled artifice and engineering, and their naval armada is a formidable force to be reckoned with.  
  
One of the main factions in Tuligaña is the Liga de la Paz, a group of pacifist intellectuals and artists who advocate for non-violent solutions to conflicts. Based in the city of [[Cartagena]] on the southern coast of Tuligaña, the Liga de la Paz is known for its commitment to dialogue and diplomacy, and for its support of the arts and sciences. The Liga de la Paz is made up of a diverse group of people, including humans, elves, and other races, and is known for its inclusivity and its open-minded approach.  
  

Another major faction in Tuligaña is the Hermandad del Hierro, a group of militaristic nationalists who believe in the superiority of Tuligañero culture and seek to protect it at all costs. Based in the city of [[Valencia]] on the western coast of Tuligaña, the Hermandad del Hierro is known for its strong ties to the Tuligañero military and its aggressive stance towards perceived threats to the nation. The Hermandad del Hierro is made up mostly of humans, with a smaller number of dwarves and other races, and is known for its exclusivity and its strict hierarchy. Despite their different ideologies, the Liga de la Paz and the Hermandad del Hierro both have a strong presence in Tuligaña, and their tensions often shape the course of national politics.

  

To the east of the mainland, the Maruellan Sea is home to a population of French-inspired Elves who hold claim to the region. These Elves are known for their refined and elegant culture, valuing sophistication and grace. They are also skilled seafarers and traders, and their island of [[Charabia]] is a hub of commerce and cultural exchange. Despite their calm and refined exterior, the Elves of [[Charabia]] are fiercely protective of their home and will defend it with all their might if necessary.

### Tuligañeros (Too-lee-gan-yer-ohs)

These are the people associated with Tuligaña. The nation boasts an incredibly diverse array of fashions, cultures, and people, and is most accepting of non-human ancestries. Elves, Halflings, Ratfolk, and many more make their home here, as do many that are touched by extraplanar realms.

## Buwaya (Boo-why-ah)

This is the land most associated with seafaring nomads, especially inspired by South East Asia and the Philippines. The capital of Buwaya, [[Ru Ay]], is known for its loose executive hold over the many islands that make up the nation. While the government in [[Ru Ay]] may have some influence, each island is largely self-governed and the Wayanas value their independence. The city of [[Na Keung]], known as the "Blood Capital of the World," is infamous for its pirate havens and the skullduggery that takes place within its borders. It is a place where anything can be bought and sold, and the presence of sellswords and other unsavory characters is common. Despite this, [[Na Keung]] is also a hub of trade and commerce, with a large volume of meatstuffs being exported from its docks.  
  
Further to the southwest, another well-known city in Buwaya is the port of [[Kajang]]. [[Kajang]] is known for its bustling fish market and vibrant nightlife, as well as its strategic position as a hub for trade and commerce. The city is home to a diverse population, with a mix of merchants, fishermen, and pirates coexisting in a sometimes uneasy peace. [[Kajang]] is also home to the headquarters of the infamous Red Hand pirate gang, known for their daring raids and bold attacks on merchant vessels.  
  

Located off the eastern coast of the main island, the island of [[Talisay]] is home to the city of the same name. [[Talisay]] is a center of learning and culture, with a number of prestigious universities and libraries located within its borders. The city is also known for its beautiful gardens and parks, as well as its thriving arts scene. Despite its reputation as a more refined and civilized place, [[Talisay]] is not without its own dangers, with a number of criminal organizations operating in the shadows.

  

### Wayanas (Why-ahn-as)

These are the people who call Buwaya home. The Wayanas are a fiercely independent and self-sufficient people, with a long tradition of seafaring and trade. They are known for their nomadic lifestyle, traveling from island to island in search of resources and new opportunities. Despite their reputation as pirates and smugglers, the Wayanas are also skilled navigators and traders, and their naval expertise is highly sought after. Elves and Humans are most common, but the many islands bear diverse peoples, such as Fetchlings, Grippli, Lizardfolk, and those touched by the Elements.

## Kamoali’i (Kah-moe-ah-lee-ee)

The Moalian people are proud descendants of the island nation of Kamoali'i, known for its Polynesian and Hawaiian influences. Recently gained independence from Buwaya, Kamoali'i is ruled by the divine warrior king, Moi Kai, who is rumored to have been summoned from the ocean to bring peace to the islands. The Moalian people are fiercely loyal to their nation and its leader, and will go to great lengths to defend their independence.  
  
One of the most influential factions in Kamoali'i is the Hui Nalu, a group of skilled surfers and watermen who are respected throughout the nation for their courage and their connection to the ocean. Based in the city of [[Kekaha]] on the eastern island of Kamoali'i, the Hui Nalu are known for their bravery and their skill in navigating the treacherous waves of the Wai Nui Ocean. The Hui Nalu are also known for their strong sense of community and their commitment to preserving the traditional Moalian way of life.

  

Another major faction in Kamoali'i is the Ka Lae, a group of powerful and influential land-owners and business leaders. The Ka Lae are based in the city of [[Hana]] on the western island of Kamoali'i, and are infamous for their wealth and their connections to the government. The Ka Lae are driven by a strong sense of ambition and a desire for power, and are willing to do whatever it takes to achieve their goals. However, they are also known for their philanthropy and their commitment to the well-being of their community.

  

### Moalian (Mo-ah-lee-an)

The Moalian population is diverse, with a significant presence of Dwarves, Elves, Humans, and Sharkfolk. Kamoali'i is known for its rich culture and traditions, with a strong emphasis on community and harmony with nature. The Moalian people value their close-knit relationships and place a high importance on family and community. They are skilled sailors and traders, with a storied history of naval expertise in long voyages.

## Chuuren (Chew-rin)

Chuuren is a proud and enigmatic nation, with a long history of isolationism and naval defense. Inspired by East Asian cultures, particularly Chinese and Japanese, the Renjin are known for their strong naval displays and their difficulty in making contact with outsiders. Those who do manage to establish trade connections with the Renjin, however, can make significant profits.

  

Currently, the Renjin nation is facing a civil war, with a coup attempt underway in the capital city of Zhongyang. The Empress of Chuuren has considered closing all international ports in response to the unrest. One of the main factions vying for power in Chuuren is the Yellow Dragon clan, a powerful and influential noble family with strong ties to the military. The Yellow Dragons are based in the city of [[Huangse]], a major port and naval stronghold on the eastern coast of Chuuren’s mainland. The clan is known for its wealth, its martial prowess, and its ambition, and is rumored to have connections to secret societies and forbidden arts.

  

Another major faction in Chuuren is the Red Phoenix clan, a wealthy and influential merchant family with a network of trading connections throughout the region. The Red Phoenix clan is based in the city of [[Kani]], a major commercial hub on the southern coast of Chuuren’s peninsula. The clan is known for its shrewd business acumen and its willingness to take risks in pursuit of profit. However, it is also rumored to have ties to criminal organizations and to engage in less than ethical business practices. Overall, Chuuren is a nation in the midst of great political upheaval, with powerful factions vying for control and the future of the nation hanging in the balance.

  

### Renjin (Rin-gin)

 The Renjin people are diverse, with a significant population of Tengu residing in the mountainous regions of the nation. Goblins, Gnomes, and Kitsune are also common among the Renjin population. Despite their internal conflicts, the Renjin people are fiercely loyal to their nation and will do whatever it takes to protect it.

## Okaiyo (Oh-kai-yo)

Okaiyo is a diverse and multifaceted nation, hailing from the Confederacy of 5 distinct states. Inspired by the Maghreb region of Africa, particularly Morocco, Okaiyo is a land of vast deserts, coastal tropics, ore-rich mountain ranges, lush jungles, and savannahs. Each of the 5 states is ruled by a Mfalme, or Warrior-King.  
  
One of the most influential factions in Okaiyo is the merchant guild known as the Bantu Trading Company. Based in the coastal city of [[Durango]], the Bantu Trading Company controls much of the trade and commerce in Okaiyo, and has a significant presence in the other states as well. The Bantu Trading Company is known for its strict codes of conduct and its fair treatment of its employees and partners. However, it is also ruthless in its pursuit of profit, and is not afraid to use its influence and resources to bend the rules to its advantage.

  

Another major faction in Okaiyo is the religious order known as the Brotherhood of the Setting Sun. This group is based in the desert state of [[Shambhala]], and is dedicated to the worship of the sun god Khnum. The Brotherhood is a powerful and influential force in [[Shambhala]], and has a strong presence in the other states as well. The Brotherhood is known for its strict adherence to tradition and its strict codes of conduct, and is widely respected for its spiritual guidance and its charitable work. However, the Brotherhood is also fiercely protective of its power and its beliefs, and is not afraid to use force to defend them.

  

### Okitu (Oh-kee-too)

The Okitu people are divided into many different factions and groups, each with their own unique traditions and customs. The Kifuni, for example, are known for their facial coverings, which denote their social station. Lower class merchants may wear hijab or similar cloth scarves, while higher class nobles and warriors wear metal masks, with the complexity of the design and rarity of the metal indicating their power. The State of [[Ardhi]] is home to a vast underground metropolis of Dwelves, while the general Okitu population also includes significant numbers of Strix (Vulture-People), Anadi (Spider-People), and Kashrishi (Rhino-People). Despite their diversity, the Okitu people are united in their loyalty to their nation and its rulers.

## Na’Drukk (Nah-Drook’)

Na'Drukk is a land inspired by Dutch colonial aggression, with cultural influences from Sweden, Russia, Ireland, and England. The capital city of [[Lesny]] is a sylvan fortress nestled in a fjord valley, serving as the seat of power for the ruler, [[Kral Jadon]]. Much of the continent is dominated by mountains and tundra, with moorlands and temperate forests located closer to the southern end.  
  
One of the main factions in Na'Drukk is the Vrijheid Bond, a group of liberal reformers who seek to modernize and liberalize the nation. Based in the city of [[Petrodvorets]] on the western coast of Na'Drukk, the Vrijheid Bond is known for its support of individual freedoms and its opposition to authoritarianism. The Vrijheid Bond is made up of a diverse group of people, including humans, dwarves, and other races, and is known for its inclusivity and its commitment to equality.

  

Another major faction in Na'Drukk is the Kranzler Kreis, a group of traditionalist conservatives who seek to preserve the traditional values and culture of Na'Drukk. Based in the city of [[Rostock]] on the eastern coast of Na'Drukk, the Kranzler Kreis is known for its support of a strong centralized government and its opposition to foreign influence. The Kranzler Kreis is made up mostly of humans, with a smaller number of dwarves and other races, and is known for its exclusivity and its strict adherence to tradition. Despite their different ideologies, the Vrijheid Bond and the Kranzler Kreis both have a strong presence in Na'Drukk, and their tensions often shape the course of national politics.

  

### Droog (Droog!)

The population of Na'Drukk, known as the Droog, is unique in that the majority of the cities are dominated by individuals of nearly-identical ancestry, with Humans and Dwarfs making up the majority. Large numbers of other races are not common, and tend to form smaller settlements along the coast. The largest minority group in Na'Drukk is the Orcs, who are scattered across the edges of the continent. While the Droog may have a reputation for aggression and colonization, most are also fiercely loyal to their nation and its ruler, [[Kral Jadon]]. The Droog value strength and determination, and are known for their resilient and hardy nature.

## The Maelstrom

At the northernmost reaches of the Kalago Ocean lies an ever-roiling storm spanning hundreds of miles. Time, space, and sanity all seem to warp around its presence as the sky and sea both crack to the mercurial whim of the storm.

### Legends

There are many names and legends surrounding [[The Maelstrom]]. Some refer to it as The Herald, tying in the tale that it is a harbinger of the end of times and will slowly consume the entire planet. One of the most famous legends of [[The Maelstrom]] refers to its Eye: wizened sailors and lunatics alike tell the tale of endless treasure and eternal life that remains hidden in the Eye of [[The Maelstrom]]. Many captains, having reached the end of their journeys by old age or otherwise, send themselves through [[The Maelstrom]] to rip apart their ship in the slim hope that they will survive to see a piece of paradise.  
  
Other legends of [[The Maelstrom]] spin it as a doorway to other dimensions, and that those who enter it are transported to other realms beyond the physical world. Some say that [[The Maelstrom]] is home to powerful and ancient beings, who use it to travel between worlds and manipulate events on different planes of existence. Some believe that [[The Maelstrom]] is a manifestation of the collective unconscious, and that those who enter it are able to tap into the deepest and most hidden parts of their own minds. Despite the many legends and stories surrounding [[The Maelstrom]], one thing is certain: it is a place of great danger and mystery, and few who venture into its depths return unchanged. Whether it is a gateway to other worlds, a harbinger of the end times, or something else entirely, [[The Maelstrom]] remains one of the most enigmatic and enigmatic forces in the known world.

## Ship-Spirits

People have always personified great ships, assigning them personalities and revering them for famous deeds. Somehow, strange occult magic has created the Mavishan Crystals, vessels that take the idea of a ship and give it a physical life, often in humanoid form.  
  
Only a handful of ships in each nation's navy have made use of these little-understood Crystals, but in popular understanding these ship-spirits give the captain and crew the ability to speak with their vessel, and she in turn can help them with repairs and steering. Part mascot, part majordomo, ship-spirits unsettle some, triggering the same uncanny anxiety as the thought of a child’s toy coming to life, but sailors seem to cherish them, and they have captured the popular imagination in novel, song, and theater.

### Mavishan Crystals

A marvelous creation of life cannot stem from nothing. Throughout a ship's life, moments are recorded in the stomp of boots that walk its decks, the blood and liquor spilled, the sweat dropped from fights and …other activities. Given enough time and energy, the memories eventually coalesce into a[[Mavishan Crystals Lore|| Mavishan Crystal]].  
  
Over the course of a one-hour ritual aboard a ship while it is in motion, the Mavishan Crystal gathers up the psychic energies of every single hope, dream, and fear related to the vessel, then weaves them into a Ship-Spirit. The result is an intelligent creature of thoughtflesh, driven to loyally serve her nation and inspire her crew and officers with purpose and conviction. As ships are generally thought of as feminine, a Ship-Spirit usually appears as a glamorous humanoid woman, almost like a figurehead brought to life. The Ship-Spirit can be recalled and summoned by embedding the Mavishan Crystal into the woodwork of the vessel.  
  
Bard tales often tell of the great feats accomplished by ships with Ship-Spirits. One legend tells of the ghostly ship "The Way of the Winds," which sailed through a raging storm and emerged unscathed, thanks to the guidance of its Ship-Spirit. Another legend speaks of the "Eternal Voyage," a ship that sailed off the edge of the world and returned with tales of strange and wondrous lands beyond. These legends have captured the imaginations of sailors and land-dwellers alike, and many dream of one day sailing on a ship with a Mavishan Crystal of their own.
