At the northernmost reaches of the Kalago Ocean lies an ever-roiling storm spanning hundreds of miles. Time, space, and sanity all seem to warp around its presence as the sky and sea both crack to the mercurial whim of the storm.

### Legends

There are many names and legends surrounding The Maelstrom. Some refer to it as The Herald, tying in the tale that it is a harbinger of the end of times and will slowly consume the entire planet. One of the most famous legends of The Maelstrom refers to its Eye: wizened sailors and lunatics alike tell the tale of endless treasure and eternal life that remains hidden in the Eye of the Maelstrom. Many captains, having reached the end of their journeys by old age or otherwise, send themselves through the Maelstrom to rip apart their ship in the slim hope that they will survive to see a piece of paradise.  
  
Other legends of The Maelstrom spin it as a doorway to other dimensions, and that those who enter it are transported to other realms beyond the physical world. Some say that the Maelstrom is home to powerful and ancient beings, who use it to travel between worlds and manipulate events on different planes of existence. Some believe that the Maelstrom is a manifestation of the collective unconscious, and that those who enter it are able to tap into the deepest and most hidden parts of their own minds. Despite the many legends and stories surrounding The Maelstrom, one thing is certain: it is a place of great danger and mystery, and few who venture into its depths return unchanged. Whether it is a gateway to other worlds, a harbinger of the end times, or something else entirely, The Maelstrom remains one of the most enigmatic and enigmatic forces in the known world.
