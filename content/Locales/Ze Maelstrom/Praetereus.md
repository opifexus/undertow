
The Praetereus was a multiverse-wide spell that was cast by [[Alassabouros]] after the incident with [[The Man Lost To Time]]: a blessing to the wind and the waves from the Occult Weave... a way to make sure there will always be bountiful souls to harvest. Mavishan Crystals. The secret to them is held within the Eye, at Oribos. He learned this from the Algothlus. Politicians have discovered this "relationship" and deal, now secret societies are commissioning the machine- a way to keep the gods AND mortals in check by destroying the secret to eternal souls. Mavishan Crystals can be consumed to lengthen life.

The spell commands that all living creatures cannot speak information about the Oribos, the Praetereus, or the magics that created the [[Mavishan Crystals Lore| Mavishan Crystals]] .
As a contingency to protect the information, the Praetereus also created The [[Arbiters]] to guard Oribos and the secrets within. This was created in collaboration between [[Alassabouros]] and [[The Pathfinder Society|Abadar]].
