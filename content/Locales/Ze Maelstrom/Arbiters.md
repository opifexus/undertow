Arbiters or Mavishans are life-like entities that guard the thingy under [[The Maelstrom]]. They were created as a failsafe to ensure that the secrets of [[The Man Lost To Time]] were not also lost to time.

Since they are technically not life, they are not bound by the [[Praetereus]].
