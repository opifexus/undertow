[[Krajcik]] is a Naga from The Under, a city below [[Ardhi]]. They have powers to form Arcane Bargains in exchange for gold or information. Krajcik has been a criminal informant for [[Chief Investigator Lazaro| Lazaro]] for the past few months due to unrest in The Under.

### Arcane Bargain with [[Lyra]] 
 In Session Beta [[2 - Arcane Bargain]] , Lyra struck an Arcane Bargain with [[Krajcik]] in order to gather more information about [[Mother]].
