[[Reiser]], Head Priest of the Lady of Graves, is a Drow that leads the Temple of Pharasma in [[Ardhi]].  

Reiser works closely with individuals from [[The Pathfinder Society]], and by proxy, the Bantu Trading Company. His point of contact in [[Durango]] is [[Rivington]], although he has seen [[Kifaru]] and [[Mwenye]] before.
