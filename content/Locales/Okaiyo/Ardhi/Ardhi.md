Okaiyo is a diverse and multifaceted nation, hailing from the Confederacy of 5 distinct states. Inspired by the Maghreb region of Africa, particularly Morocco, Okaiyo is a land of vast deserts, coastal tropics, ore-rich mountain ranges, lush jungles, and savannahs. Each of the 5 states is ruled by a Mfalme, or Warrior-King. 

The Okitu people are divided into many different factions and groups, each with their own unique traditions and customs. The Kifuni, for example, are known for their facial coverings, which denote their social station. Lower class merchants may wear hijab or similar cloth scarves, while higher class nobles and warriors wear metal masks, with the complexity of the design and rarity of the metal indicating their power. The State of Ardhi is home to a vast underground metropolis of Dwelves, while the general Okitu population also includes significant numbers of Strix (Vulture-People), Anadi (Spider-People), and Kashrishi (Rhino-People).
