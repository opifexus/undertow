One of the most influential factions in Okaiyo is the merchant guild known as the Bantu Trading Company. Based in the coastal city of Durango, the Bantu Trading Company controls much of the trade and commerce in Okaiyo, and has a significant presence in the other states as well. The Bantu Trading Company is known for its strict codes of conduct and its fair treatment of its employees and partners.
