Another major faction in Okaiyo is the religious order known as the Brotherhood of the Setting Sun. This group is based in the desert state of Shambhala, and is dedicated to the worship of the sun god Khnum. The Brotherhood is a powerful and influential force in Shambhala, and has a strong presence in the other states as well. The Brotherhood is known for its strict adherence to tradition and its strict codes of conduct, and is widely respected for its spiritual guidance and its charitable work. However, the Brotherhood is also fiercely protective of its power and its beliefs, and is not afraid to use force to defend them.
