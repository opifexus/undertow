### The Trial of Tempest and Tide: One-Shot Adventure Outline

#### **Adventure Overview**

The party arrives at the Temple of Gozreh, located in a bustling port town. Here, they must face the Trial of Mind, known as the Trial of Tempest and Tide. This trial will test their understanding of Gozreh's dual nature and their ability to manipulate the elements of wind and water to navigate a complex tiered maze.

#### **Part 1: Arrival at the Temple**

1. **Introduction:**
    
    - The players arrive at the port town of Windward Harbor, known for its close ties to the deity Gozreh.
    - The temple is a magnificent structure, half built into the cliffside overlooking the sea, with intricate carvings of storms and waves adorning its walls.
2. **Meeting the High Priest:**
    
    - The players are greeted by High Priestess Elara, who explains the importance of the Trial of Tempest and Tide.
    - She provides background on Gozreh, emphasizing the deity’s dual nature as both the storm and the surf.
3. **Entering the Temple:**
    
    - The party is led to the entrance of the trial chamber, a large, ornate door inscribed with symbols of wind and water.
    - Elara warns them that only by understanding and balancing these elements will they succeed.

#### **Part 2: The Trial Chamber**

1. **Chamber Description:**
    
    - The chamber is a vast, multi-tiered room with platforms at different heights, interconnected by various mechanisms.
    - Each tier is themed around an aspect of Gozreh’s nature, with murals and decorations depicting scenes of storms and ocean waves.
    - At the center of the room are two large control panels, one marked with symbols of wind and the other with symbols of water.
2. **Elemental Control Panels:**
    
    - The wind control panel has levers, dials, and buttons that can summon various intensities of wind, from gentle breezes to fierce gales.
    - The water control panel allows the players to create different forms of water, such as gentle rain, heavy downpours, and rushing currents.

#### **Part 3: The Tiered Maze**

1. **Maze Structure:**
    
    - The maze consists of three main tiers, each with its own set of challenges and mechanisms.
    - Each tier must be navigated using a combination of wind and water controls, requiring cooperation between party members.
2. **Tier 1: The Garden of Gozreh**
    
    - **Description:** A lush garden with plants that need gentle rain to thrive.
    - **Objective:** Use the water control panel to create a gentle rain that revives the garden and reveals hidden pathways to the next level.
    - **Mechanism:** One player must operate the water panel while others navigate the garden, avoiding over-watering and potential floods.
3. **Tier 2: The Wind Currents**
    
    - **Description:** A series of platforms that can only be reached by creating updrafts of wind.
    - **Objective:** Use the wind control panel to generate updrafts that lift party members to higher platforms.
    - **Mechanism:** One player must control the wind panel, coordinating with others to time the updrafts correctly to avoid being thrown off balance or missing platforms.
4. **Tier 3: The Flooded Passage**
    
    - **Description:** A series of interconnected tunnels and passages filled with water.
    - **Objective:** Use a combination of wind and water controls to navigate the flooded passages, clearing obstacles and moving to the final platform.
    - **Mechanism:** Players must communicate to control the flow of water and the direction of wind currents, ensuring safe passage through the tunnels.

#### **Part 4: The Final Challenge**

1. **Central Mechanism:**
    
    - **Description:** At the top of the maze, the players find a central mechanism that requires simultaneous control of both wind and water to activate.
    - **Objective:** The players must work together to balance the elements, creating a harmonious flow that activates the final mechanism.
    - **Mechanism:** One player operates the wind controls while another manages the water controls, coordinating their actions to achieve the correct balance. The remaining players assist by solving smaller puzzles or providing guidance on timing.
2. **Final Activation:**
    
    - Once the central mechanism is activated, a serene light fills the chamber, signifying the completion of the trial.

#### **Part 5: Conclusion**

1. **Victory and Revelation:**
    
    - Upon completing the trial, the chamber calms, and the players are bathed in a serene light.
    - The high priestess re-enters the chamber, congratulating them on their success and explaining that they have demonstrated true understanding of Gozreh’s dual nature.
2. **Reward and Prophecy:**
    
    - The players are bestowed with a blessing from Gozreh, enhancing their abilities related to wind and water.
    - Elara also provides a prophetic vision hinting at the next trial they will face in their journey.
3. **Departure:**
    
    - The party exits the temple, feeling a deeper connection to Gozreh and better prepared for the challenges ahead.
    - They leave Windward Harbor, ready to continue their spiritual journey to the next temple.

This detailed outline provides a structured and immersive experience that emphasizes the dual nature of Gozreh and challenges the players to think creatively and work together to overcome the trial.
