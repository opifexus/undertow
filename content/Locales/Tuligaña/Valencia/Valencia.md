Another major faction in Tuligaña is the Hermandad del Hierro, a group of militaristic nationalists who believe in the superiority of Tuligañero culture and seek to protect it at all costs. Based in the city of Valencia on the western coast of Tuligaña, the Hermandad del Hierro is known for its strong ties to the Tuligañero military and its aggressive stance towards perceived threats to the nation. The Hermandad del Hierro is made up mostly of humans, with a smaller number of dwarves and other races, and is known for its exclusivity and its strict hierarchy. Despite their different ideologies, the Liga de la Paz and the Hermandad del Hierro both have a strong presence in Tuligaña, and their tensions often shape the course of national politics.

Valecosta and Valevalle
