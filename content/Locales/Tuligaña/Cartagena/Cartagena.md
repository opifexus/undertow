One of the main factions in Tuligaña is the Liga de la Paz, a group of pacifist intellectuals and artists who advocate for non-violent solutions to conflicts. Based in the city of Cartagena on the southern coast of Tuligaña, the Liga de la Paz is known for its commitment to dialogue and diplomacy, and for its support of the arts and sciences. The Liga de la Paz is made up of a diverse group of people, including humans, elves, and other races, and is known for its inclusivity and its open-minded approach.
