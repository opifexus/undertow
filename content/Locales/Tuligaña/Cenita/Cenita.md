The Capital Cenita

This is the land associated with the Spanish and Portuguese Naval Armadas. While their government in Cenita may want to claim they own the seas, the Tuligañeros are much more relaxed.  The Tuligañeros value innovation and creativity, valuing those who can bring new ideas and inventions to the table. They have a reputation for being relaxed and accepting, welcoming people of all backgrounds and ancestries into their society. The Tuligañeros are known for their skilled artifice and engineering, and their naval armada is a formidable force to be reckoned with.
