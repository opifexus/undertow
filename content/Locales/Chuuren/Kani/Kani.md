Another major faction in Chuuren is the Red Phoenix clan, a wealthy and influential merchant family with a network of trading connections throughout the region. The Red Phoenix clan is based in the city of Kani, a major commercial hub on the southern coast of Chuuren’s peninsula. The clan is known for its shrewd business acumen and its willingness to take risks in pursuit of profit. However, it is also rumored to have ties to criminal organizations and to engage in less than ethical business practices.
