Currently, the Renjin nation is facing a civil war, with a coup attempt underway in the capital city of Zhongyang. The Empress of Chuuren has considered closing all international ports in response to the unrest. One of the main factions vying for power in Chuuren is the Yellow Dragon clan, a powerful and influential noble family with strong ties to the military. The Yellow Dragons are based in the city of Huangse, a major port and naval stronghold on the eastern coast of Chuuren’s mainland.
