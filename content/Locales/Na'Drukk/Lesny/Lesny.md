Na'Drukk is a land inspired by Dutch colonial aggression, with cultural influences from Sweden, Russia, Ireland, and England. The capital city of Lesny is a sylvan fortress nestled in a fjord valley, serving as the seat of power for the ruler, Kral Jadon. Much of the continent is dominated by mountains and tundra, with moorlands and temperate forests located closer to the southern end.
