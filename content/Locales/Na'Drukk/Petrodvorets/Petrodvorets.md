One of the main factions in Na'Drukk is the Vrijheid Bond, a group of liberal reformers who seek to modernize and liberalize the nation. Based in the city of Petrodvorets on the western coast of Na'Drukk, the Vrijheid Bond is known for its support of individual freedoms and its opposition to authoritarianism. The Vrijheid Bond is made up of a diverse group of people, including humans, dwarves, and other races, and is known for its inclusivity and its commitment to equality.
