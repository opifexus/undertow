Another major faction in Na'Drukk is the Kranzler Kreis, a group of traditionalist conservatives who seek to preserve the traditional values and culture of Na'Drukk. Based in the city of Rostock on the eastern coast of Na'Drukk, the Kranzler Kreis is known for its support of a strong centralized government and its opposition to foreign influence. The Kranzler Kreis is made up mostly of humans, with a smaller number of dwarves and other races, and is known for its exclusivity and its strict adherence to tradition.
